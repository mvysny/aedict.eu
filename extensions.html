Companion apps

Aedict Reader Popup

Aedict3 Reader Popup is a floating window which analyses Japanese sentence copied to a clipboard. The window hovers over other applications, allowing you to seamlessly look up words and kanjis, without getting distracted by closing the document you are reading.

Now part of Aedict Ultimate

These used to be separate apps. They are now built into Aedict itself and activated by the Aedict Ultimate subscription; there is nothing extra to install.

Kanjipad Extension

A more accurate kanjipad. It knows a lot more kanjis than the internal kanjipad, and its stroke order and stroke count requirements are not as strict.

KanjiDraw Practice

Stroke order animation for 7000+ kanjis: click the Stroke Order Diagram and the animation will start. You can practise your own kanji drawing, with the stroke order diagram and/or the kanji contours to help you draw a kanji for the first time.

Discontinued

Aedict OCR

A stand-alone OCR (optical character recognition) app for Japanese text, Tategaki included. It is no longer developed.

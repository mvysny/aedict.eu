--- title: "Privacy" ---

Privacy Policy

Aedict is an offline dictionary. It collects no analytics, has no crash reporting, shows no ads, and sends your data nowhere except where you tell it to. This page lists every time Aedict goes online and what it sends.

Aedict is published by Martin Vysný, Finland, who is responsible for the data described here. Contact: support@aedict.eu.

Dictionary Downloads

Aedict downloads dictionaries, and checks for dictionary updates, from the aedict-online.eu server. Aedict sends nothing that identifies you; the server sees only what every web request carries, such as your IP address. The server keeps no log of these requests.

Aedict Online

Only when you ask it to, Aedict synchronizes your notepad, SRS and recently viewed entries with your account on the Aedict Online server, so that your other devices see them too. Aedict signs in with the username and password you chose. The server stores your account and the synchronized data, nothing else.

The server keeps no log of your requests. Its technical logs record when a device connects for synchronization (your account's internal ID and the time, not your IP address), the internal sync token created with your sync account (not your password), and the server's errors. They are not shared, and they are kept for at most 90 days.

To delete your account and all of its data, e-mail support@aedict.eu.

Purchases

Aedict Ultimate is bought through Google Play. Aedict learns only which products you own, never your name, payment details or e-mail. Google's handling of your payment data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Everything Else Stays on Your Phone

Your notepad, SRS, search history and settings stay on your device. Exporting to AnkiDroid hands the cards to the AnkiDroid app on the same device. Links to online dictionaries and other web pages open in your browser, under those sites' own privacy policies.

Your Rights

Under the GDPR you may ask what data Aedict Online holds about you, have it corrected or deleted, or receive a copy: write to support@aedict.eu. You may also complain to a data protection authority; in Finland that is the Data Protection Ombudsman.

Changes

When this policy changes, the new version is published on this page, with its date.
